# Universe Lore — STARVEIL

## 🌌 The Sector: Khepri & Helios Clusters
Set in 2488 AD. Human civilization in the outer rim is divided between corporate megastructures, independent frontier colonies, and shadow criminal syndicates.

### Factions
1. **Aegis Dynamics:** Corporate defense megacorporation controlling orbital shipyards.
2. **Free Colonies Coalition:** Frontier alliance of independent planetary settlements.
3. **Vaelen Syndicate:** Smuggling and black-market criminal syndicate.
4. **The Transcendent Mind:** Cyber-monk enclave seeking post-human AI integration.

### Act I Storyline: Signal in the Dark
The science vessel *Valkyrie* went silent in orbit above gas giant Khepri IV, transmitting quantum telemetry harmonics pointing toward an ancient precursor AI sub-mind core.
